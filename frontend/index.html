Heart rate per year: average resting · max (bpm)
resting: lower = fitter
Max: the highest heart rate held for 10 s in a run that year (click a point to open it). Readings above 220 − age/2 are treated as watch glitches, and so is one run more than 10 bpm above every other.
Aerobic efficiency (pace at 140 bpm)
faster = fitter
Aerobic efficiency: for every flat easy run (≥5 km, ≥30 min, under 10 m of climbing per km, steady heart rate), its speed is normalised to 140 bpm with your own speed-vs-heart-rate slope; the year's value is the median of those runs, odd ones (HR glitches, snow…) left out. Same as on the Body tab.
